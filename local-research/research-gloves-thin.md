Checked 22.11.2026, 01:00–09:55. Full data, 24 offers: `research-gloves-thin.json`.

**The brief.** Two pairs: a man in L, and a woman who is S in most brands. Each glove should be about as slim as the Van Rysel EDR "jesienne" but warm near 0 °C in dry weather. It must have no velcro strap, no rubber ribs, no reflective stripes, and no ski or work-glove look.

**Ranking rule.**
1. A real Black Friday discount: the price is below the shop's Omnibus 30-day low, or a working code applies.
2. Cheapest total delivered to Katowice.
3. Quality and fit.

Before ranking, I dropped gloves that fail the brief on looks or warmth. They are listed further down.

**How the looks were checked.**
- For each glove in the table, someone opened the photo URL and looked at it: the main agent did so for Salomon, Viking, Mammut, GOREWEAR, Chiba and Etip, and the shop agents did so for the rest.
- The "Look (from photo)" column describes only what is visible in that photo or written on the shop page.

## Recommendation

**Cheapest: Viking Windcross at CentrumRowerowe, 109.99 zł per pair, 219.98 zł for both, delivery free.**
- By the strict rule the Salomon Cross Warm is first, 4 zł cheaper per pair. But nothing shows it is warmer than the EDR jesienne at 0 °C: it has no membrane and no insulation.
- Windcross has a windproof GORE-TEX INFINIUM shell, and two buyers report warm hands at about −2…+5 °C and down to about −5 °C. Several buyers also call it thin.
- The trade-off is looks. The photo shows a tall ribbed knit cuff and a leather palm patch, so it is not as smooth as EDR jesienne.
- If the woman wants the smoothest look and mostly walks, the Salomon at 105.98 is the alternative.
- Windcross sizes: Viking's chart puts size 9 at 22.5–24 cm and size 7 at 18–20 cm.
- The price has no Black Weeks badge and no end date. It is a "Superoferta" below the 30-day low.

**Best quality: Mammut Astro Light SO at MMT, 231.20 zł per pair with BLACK20 (until 30.11), 462.40 zł for both, delivery free.**
- In the photo it is the closest to EDR jesienne: smooth black stretch, a small logo, a plain cuff.
- It adds a windproof GORE WINDSTOPPER membrane at 55 g. Mammut rates warmth 3/6, so at 0 °C it suits walking more than fast cycling.
- The only discount is the code. The page data also holds an older 30-day low of 215 zł (3.08.2026) that is below the code price.
